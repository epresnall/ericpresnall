## 1) What resume to create for on-camera roles

**The big finding: you need ONE strong ATS-safe master resume, then light per-role tailoring.**
On-camera work is unusual — it's half "creative talent" (reel matters most) and half "normal
job application" (goes through an ATS that a robot reads first). Your resume has to win BOTH:

**ATS rules (the robot gate — research-backed):**
- **Single column. No tables, text boxes, headers/footers, or graphics.** ATS parsers scramble
  multi-column and boxed layouts — your info lands in the wrong field or vanishes. `resume.md`
  is built single-column for exactly this.
- **Standard section headings** ("Professional Summary," "Experience," "Skills," "Education").
  Cute headings ("My Story") confuse parsers.
- **Match the job's keywords.** ATS ranks you on keyword overlap (aim ~60–80% of the role's
  key terms). That's why `resume.md` has a dense **Core Skills & Keywords** line — mirror the
  posting's exact words (if they say "on-camera talent," use that phrase, not just "presenter").
- **Standard file:** upload a **.docx** (most compatible) unless they ask for PDF. Name it
  `Eric-Presnall-On-Camera-Spokesperson.docx`.
- **No "keyword stuffing" tricks** (white text, hidden keywords) — Workday/Greenhouse/Lever
  flag it and it reads as fraud.

**On-camera specifics (the human gate — what makes YOU the pick):**
- **Lead with a title + summary that names the exact role type** (spokesperson/presenter/host).
- **Quantify results** the way you'd pitch a client: ROAS, views, conversion, hours saved.
- **The reel link goes at the top** — for talent, the reel is the real resume. The document
  gets you past the robot; the reel gets you hired.
- **Keep credits relevant-first:** on-camera experience up top, stage/screen as supporting
  proof of range, not the headline.

## 2) Should you customize/tailor your resume per job? — YES (but smartly)

The research is decisive: **tailored resumes get ~6x more interviews** (Teal data), and
recruiters can tell in seconds when a resume was mass-blasted. But you do NOT rewrite from
scratch each time. The efficient system:

**Keep `resume.md` as your master. Per role (~10–15 min), tweak only 3 things:**
1. **The summary's first line** — echo the role's exact title ("On-Camera Video Host" vs
   "Video Spokesperson" vs "UGC Creator") and their industry.
2. **The Core Skills & Keywords line** — reorder/insert so the terms THEY use appear first
   and verbatim (this is what moves your ATS score).
3. **The top 1–2 bullets / proof point** — surface the result that matches their bucket
   (funnel → ROAS; content → views; product → hours saved; general → the 500+/Top 1% line).

Everything else stays fixed. This gets you the 6x lift without the time cost. **Rule of thumb:**
tailor for every role you genuinely want; don't tailor for long-shots you're spray-applying to
(better to skip those entirely — they lower your hit rate and waste your time).

**The workflow, end to end, per job:**
1. Paste the JD into the AI with `resume.md` → ask it to output the 3 tailored tweaks.
2. Paste the JD into `cover-letter-template.md`'s structure → get the "about them" letter.
3. Paste the JD + screening questions into `ai-prompt-screening-answers.md` → get answers.
4. Drop your matching reel/bucket clip (from `portfolio-reel-guide.md`).
5. Submit. ~15 minutes, fully tailored, accurate.

## 4) Which jobs to search out (titles, boards, filters)

**Search these titles (rotate them — postings use different words for the same job):**
- On-Camera Host · On-Camera Presenter · On-Camera Talent
- Video Spokesperson · Brand Spokesperson · Brand Ambassador (video)
- Video Presenter · Video Host · YouTube Host / YouTube Presenter
- UGC Creator · Content Creator (on-camera) · Creator in Residence
- Live Shopping Host · Livestream Host · QVC-style host
- Video Content Lead / Video Producer **(only if on-camera is in the JD** — many are behind-camera, skip those)

**Where to search (off-Upwork):**
- **LinkedIn Jobs** — 386+ "video spokesperson" and 850+ "video presenter" postings live now.
  Set alerts for each title, Remote + US.
- **Greenhouse / Ashby / Lever / Workable** — company ATS boards where brands post
  "on-camera creator / video host" roles (this is the territory you were missing).
- **Wellfound (AngelList)** — startups hiring their first on-camera face.
- **Seek** — for AU/APAC remote roles (you have the background).
- **Built In** — tech/startup on-camera + content roles.
- **Company career pages directly** — DTC brands, SaaS, media, edtech, health, finance.

**Filters that matter:** Remote · US business hours ok · contract OR full-time OR part-time
(you can flex). Prioritize roles that want ONE consistent face (anchor potential = your $5–15K/mo
retainer path), not one-off gigs.

**Green flags (chase these):** "dedicated on-camera host," "creator in residence," "build our
video presence," "ongoing," "series," "face of the brand." These are anchor roles.

**Red flags (skip / deprioritize):** pure editor/videographer with no on-camera, $15/hr
YouTube-presenter gigs (the market floor — beneath your rate), unpaid "exposure," or roles
that want on-site-only in a city you can't reach.

**On rate:** the low end of these titles pays ~$15–36/hr; that's the floor and not your game.
Your value is being the reliable, end-to-end, one-face-for-everything talent — that's a monthly
relationship ($5–15K/mo per `playbooks/retainer-pricing.md`), not an hourly seat. In
applications, don't anchor to their hourly number; steer to a call.
