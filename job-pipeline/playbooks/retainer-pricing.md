# Retainer pricing & packaging — how to land $5–15K/mo

The job posts are the door. This is how you walk a prospect from "one video" to a signed
**monthly retainer.** Present *packages and outcomes*, never an hourly rate.

## The three tiers (anchor high, let them choose)
Present all three; lead with **Partner**. People pick the middle when the top is visible.

### ▸ Partner — $15K/mo  ·  "your video department"
Your on-camera face across **all formats** — ads, VSL/explainer, social/short-form, product/onboarding.
~16–20 finished assets/mo · full script development · dedicated strategy + analytics review · first-priority 24–48h turnaround · full team (scripters, editors, thumbnails).
*For: funded brands / anyone who said "we need someone to own our video."*

### ▸ Growth — $10K/mo  ·  the sweet spot
Two buckets (e.g. ads + social, or product + content). ~8–12 assets/mo · full scripting + delivery · priority turnaround · monthly performance review.
*For: brands with a clear primary channel who want momentum.*

### ▸ Starter — $5K/mo  ·  the foot in the door
One bucket focus. ~4–6 assets/mo · scripting + delivery · monthly check-in. Easy to say yes to; upgrades naturally once results show.
*For: smaller brands / cautious first-timers. Get in, prove it, grow it.*

> Three of these (any mix) = the goal. One Partner + one Growth + one Starter ≈ $30K/mo.

## How to present it (on the call / in the follow-up)
1. **Diagnose first** — which bucket is their pain (convert / grow / retain)? Match the tier.
2. **Anchor at Partner:** "The way I usually work with a brand is as their whole video function — that's the Partner tier at $15K a month." Pause.
3. **Then give the ladder:** "If you want to start focused, Growth at $10K covers two channels, and Starter at $5K gets one moving." Let them choose.
4. **Justify with ROI, not effort:** "One ad that converts pays for months of this — I had a finance ad run positive ROAS for six straight months. You're not buying videos, you're buying a face your audience trusts and content that performs."
5. **De-risk:** free sample from their script first + full refund if the first is a miss. Almost nobody says no to that.
6. **Close to a start date, not a debate:** "Want me to record a free sample this week so you can see it, then we lock a start date?"

## Rules
- **Never quote hourly.** Hourly = contractor. Monthly = partner. (If they push for a rate, translate: "I package it monthly so you get the whole system, not a meter running.")
- **Always offer the free sample** — it's your unfair closer.
- **Start them, then grow them** — a $5K Starter that delivers becomes a $15K Partner in a quarter.
- **Anonymize case studies** when you name results; keep pricing out of any public video.

recurring floor is what ends feast-or-famine. **3 anchors is the whole game** — everything in
