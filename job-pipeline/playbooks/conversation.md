### The 15-minute call — steer to a retainer
1. Ask first: "What are you trying to get your videos to do — and what's not working now?" Let them talk.
2. Mirror it into a bucket (funnel / content / product / general).
3. Offer the small win: free sample from their script (zero-risk yes).
4. Paint the ongoing system (Message 3).
5. Propose a monthly shape: "Most brands start me at [X videos/month] and we grow." Name a monthly relationship, **not** an hourly rate.
6. Book the next step before you hang up.

**Golden rule:** never quote hourly — quote a monthly relationship. Hourly caps you at "contractor"; monthly is how you reach $5–15K/mo per client.
