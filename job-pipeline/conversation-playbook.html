The 15-minute call — how to steer it to a retainer

  1. Ask first: "What are you trying to get your videos to do — and what's not working right now?" Let them talk.
  2. Mirror it back into a bucket (ads/funnel · content/audience · product/training · general). Show you get it.
  3. Offer the small win: "Send me a script and I'll record a free sample so you can hear it." (Zero-risk yes.)
  4. Paint the ongoing system: use Message 3 — one person, every format, a full team behind you.
  5. Propose a simple start: "Most brands start me at [X videos/month] and we grow from there." Name a monthly shape, not an hourly rate.
  6. Book the next step before you hang up — the sample delivery or a follow-up.
Rule of thumb: never quote hourly. Quote a monthly relationship. Hourly caps you at "contractor"; monthly is how you get to $5–15K/mo per client.
