🔓 Warm-intro playbook — recently-closed roles

For companies whose on-camera posting already filled. The hidden market most applicants never touch.

Why this works: a company that just posted an on-camera role has proven demand and budget already allocated to video. Even if the seat's filled: (1) new hires don't always stick, (2) one hire rarely covers all the video a brand needs, (3) you become first-in-line for the overflow and the next opening. High signal, almost zero competition.

Message 1 · the soft "raise my hand" (LinkedIn DM or email)
Hi [Name] — saw [Company] was hiring an on-camera host for [role/show]. Whether or not that seat's filled, figured I'd raise my hand. I'm a video spokesperson and host — 20 years on camera, 500+ brand videos, Animal Planet and Broadway along the way. If you ever need overflow, a second face, or someone to take video off your plate end to end, I'd love to be on your radar. Reel 👉 https://videorep.co No pitch — just planting a flag.

Low-pressure, complimentary, memorable. You're not asking for the job they filled — you're offering to be useful.

Message 2 · the "you clearly invest in video" angle (→ VideoRep partner)
Hi [Name] — [Company]'s clearly leaning into video (saw the [role] opening). Most brands that hire one on-camera person quickly find they need more content than one role can cover — that's usually where I come in: one presenter plus a full production team, so ads, social, and product videos all come from one place. If that's ever useful, here's my reel 👉 https://videorep.co — happy to record a free sample from any script.

Positions VideoRep as the ongoing partner, not a rival to the person they just hired.

Message 3 · light follow-up (~1 week later, then stop)
Hey [Name] — just floating this back up in case it's useful down the line. Filing me under "on-camera, whenever you need it" is all I'm after. Reel's here if it helps 👉 https://videorep.co

One follow-up only. If no reply, leave it — you've planted the flag.

Who to message & how to find them

  1. Best target: the person who posted the role, or the Head of Marketing / Content / Brand / Social at the company.
  2. Find them on LinkedIn: search "[Company] marketing" or "[Company] content" — DM the most senior relevant person.
  3. No LinkedIn reply? Try [firstname]@[company].com or the marketing/careers email.
  4. Keep a simple list: company · role that closed · who you messaged · date · reply. One soft follow-up, then move on.

Example: your first warm-intro target

Penske Media (VIBE) — "On-Camera Host," closed Jan 2026.

Lead on-camera talent for editorial + branded video, artist interviews, red-carpet/festival coverage — your exact profile at a major media company. Message the VIBE / PMC content or branded-content lead on LinkedIn with Message 1. Even filled, PMC runs dozens of shows and constantly needs on-camera talent.
