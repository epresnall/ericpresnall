# Cover letter template — on-camera roles (make it ABOUT THEM)

**The #1 rule:** ~60% about THEM (their company, their product, their audience, the
problem this role solves), ~40% about Eric. Recruiters read hundreds of "I'm passionate,
I'm experienced" letters. The one that says *"here's what I see you trying to do, and here's
exactly how I'd help you do it on camera"* wins. Research backs this: naming one specific,
genuine reason you're excited about *this* company is the single biggest differentiator.

**Voice rules (same as the Upwork agent):**
- Warm, direct, human. No "Dear Hiring Manager," no "I am writing to apply for."
- Lead with THEM, not with "I."
- One or two proof points max — not the whole resume.
- End with a low-friction next step + the reel link.
- Keep it to ~150–200 words. They skim.

---

## The 4-beat structure

1. **Them first (the hook).** One specific, true observation about the company/role/product.
   Shows you actually looked.
2. **The bridge.** Connect their need → what Eric does. "You need X on camera; that's the
   thing I do all day."
3. **One proof point.** A single quantified result that matches their bucket (below).
4. **Easy next step.** Reel link + a zero-risk offer (free sample read from their script).

## Fill-in template

> Hi [First name / Team],
>
> [BEAT 1 — THEM: one specific thing about their company/product/audience/this role. e.g.
> "The way [Company] is scaling [product] through short-form video — and hiring a dedicated
> on-camera host rather than rotating freelancers — tells me you want one consistent face
> your audience actually recognizes."]
>
> [BEAT 2 — BRIDGE: that's exactly what I do. e.g. "That's the core of my work: being the
> reliable on-camera presence a brand can hand a script to and get back a finished, on-brand
> video — no chasing, no reshoots."]
>
> [BEAT 3 — ONE PROOF POINT matched to their bucket. e.g. "For one brand, a single UGC ad I
> presented ran as their top creative with positive ROAS for six months straight."]
>
> [BEAT 4 — EASY NEXT STEP. e.g. "Happy to record a short sample from one of your real
> scripts so you can see the fit before anything's decided. Reel's here: https://videorep.co
> — and I'm easy to reach at eric@ericpresnall.com."]
>
> — Eric

## Bucket → which proof point to use

Match the proof point in Beat 3 to what the role is really for:

| If the role is about… | Bucket | Use this proof point |
|---|---|---|
| Ads, VSLs, paid social, performance | **Sales Funnel** | "One UGC ad → positive ROAS 6 months straight, their best creative." OR "3x conversion, 2.28x CTR, CPA halved, ran 7+ months." |
| YouTube, brand content, audience growth | **Content Marketing** | "86,000+ organic views on a single educational video." |
| Product demos, onboarding, e-learning, training | **Product & Training** | "Saved a content team 120+ production hours/quarter by owning script-to-delivery." |
| General brand host / spokesperson / ambassador | **General Spokesperson** | "500+ five-star projects for 250+ brands incl. Microsoft, Sony, Ahrefs — Top 1% talent." |
