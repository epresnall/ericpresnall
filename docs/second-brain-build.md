## Page 2 — 🧭 Offer & Positioning — A to Z

> Settled 19 Jul 2026. Public line: **Human video for B2B tech.** One offer,
> three products. Single source of truth for what VideoRep sells.

**One-page version** — Who: marketing leaders at B2B SaaS/tech/AI companies,
20–200 people, running paid, no in-house on-camera talent. What: presenter-led
video, produced monthly, multiplied into every format. Price: $2,500 to test;
$4,500–$15,000/month ongoing.

**The hero video unit:** one script, one recording session, one primary
deliverable. A unit of production work, not a length. A 45-second ad = one
hero; a 6-minute VSL = two. This is how pricing works without a menu.

**The ladder:**

| Product | Price | What it is | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test Drive | $2,500 one-time | 1 hero + 6 assets, credited to month one | Make the first yes cost nothing |
| 511 Launch | $9,500, 10 days | 5 ads, 1 VSL, 1 confirmation video | Land big, prove fast, create the upsell |
| The Engine | Launch $4,500 · Studio $7,500 · Scale $15,000 · Partner from $20K /mo | 2/4/8/custom heroes + 12/25/50 assets | The ongoing business |

Everything else (retention libraries, e-learning, internal video) = quoted
projects for existing Engine clients. Capabilities, not offers.

**Customization rule:** fixed count, flexible composition — never discount, swap.

**Guarantee stack:** 3-month initial term then month-to-month · two revision
rounds, watermarked until approved, feedback due in 5 business days, round 3+
is a scope conversation · free presenter re-cast/re-shoot if the face doesn't
land · usage license included, raw files paid add-on or free after 6 months ·
**gated performance guarantee**: run creative 30 days at normal spend; if it
underperforms the control, free re-shoot (conditions: actually run it, real
spend, full window, shared results) · dropped: on-time-or-free.

**Downsells:** Test Drive · quarterly batch (4 heroes, $6,000, over 3 months) ·
script-only package.
**Upsells:** extra hero $1,200 · rush +30% · retention library $8–15K · second
presenter +$1,500/mo · localized + sales-enablement cuts.
**What-ifs:** founder presents → remote direction, same price · pause → one
month/year · different presenter → casting network · raw footage → paid add-on
only · going in-house → script + direction retainer $2,000/mo.

**Channel roles:** YouTube = belief · LinkedIn = meetings · **spec videos =
replies** · the Engine = what they buy.

**Spec-video weapon:** 45s of Eric presenting the prospect's own value prop,
made in ~20 min from their homepage. 20 target accounts/month.
