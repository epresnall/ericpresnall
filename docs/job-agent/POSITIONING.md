# VideoRep — Positioning & Messaging Source of Truth

> The problem Eric solves, for whom, and how we say it. All outreach copy
> (cover letters, LinkedIn, screening answers, the intro video) traces back here.
> Written 2026-07-19.

## The pain point (what's actually broken for the buyer)

B2B tech, SaaS, and AI companies are in a trust crisis with video:

1. **AI has flooded the market with generic content.** Buyers scroll past it.
   The scarce, valuable thing now is a *real human they trust* on camera.
2. **Their founders/CEOs are the obvious face — but won't or can't.** Camera-shy,
   too busy, or simply not good on camera. So the most trust-building channel
   sits unused.
3. **A full-time on-camera hire is hard to justify.** They don't need a presenter
   40 hours a week — but they do need a consistent, professional face for ads,
   explainers, demos, launches, and thought leadership.
4. **Freelance actors don't think like marketers.** They can read a script but
   don't understand conversion, funnels, or what makes a B2B viewer act. And
   AI avatars feel fake — the opposite of the trust they're trying to build.

**Net:** the highest-trust marketing channel they have (a credible human on camera)
is blocked — by discomfort, cost, and a talent gap between "actor" and "marketer."

## How Eric solves it (the mechanism)

Eric becomes their **plug-and-play on-camera presence** — the trusted face without
the full-time hire. He takes it **end-to-end**: research → script → on-camera
delivery → finished video (his team handles editing). Remote, home broadcast studio,
24–48h turnaround. They get the outcome, not a logistics project.

## The unfair advantage (why him, not anyone else)

A rare **3-in-1** that almost no one combines:
1. **Real on-camera talent** — 20+ years, Broadway tours, national commercials, TV
   hosting. Genuine presence, not a talking head.
2. **A marketer's brain** — knows what converts: ads, explainers, funnels,
   direct-response. Builds the video to *do a job*, not just look nice.
3. **Full production capability** — a team behind him, so it's the finished product.

Plus: a **psychology degree** (human connection is literally his training), global
brand credibility (Microsoft, Facebook, Ahrefs, Sony), and marketing certification.

**One line:** *"Not just a face — a marketer who happens to be a 20-year on-camera
pro, who becomes the trusted human face your brand is missing."*

## The dual play (job application = service pitch)

Every application does two jobs:
- **Primary:** apply for the on-camera **role** (part-time/remote ideal).
- **Back-door:** even if they're not hiring a full-timer, they learn VideoRep can
  give them that on-camera presence **for less than a full-time position.** A warm
  inbound lead is created either way.

## Messaging pillars (use in this order)

1. **Lead with THEM.** Their company, what they're building, the specific need in
   the posting. Prove you read it and get their world. (~60% them / 40% him.)
2. **Name the human-trust gap** in an AI-saturated market — the thing they feel.
3. **Position Eric as the fix:** the face + the marketing brain + the team,
   flexible and remote, without a full-time hire.
4. **Proof, lightly:** one relevant, real result (see wins) — never a brag dump.
5. **Low-risk CTA:** free script review / free sample from their brief → short call.

## Voice & style rules (hard rules)
- Warm, confident, calm, authentic. Value-first, slightly Hormozi-influenced.
  Conversational — a trusted expert talking to one person. Clarity over cleverness.
- **NO em dashes.** Short sentences. One idea per line.
- About their goals, not "I'm the best." Never salesy or corporate.
- Answer the 3 questions every buyer asks, up front: **is it really you on camera?**
  (yes, always) · **where are you based / can you work remote?** (Sydney, fully
  remote, US citizen) · **cost/commitment?** (frame as less than a full-time hire).
