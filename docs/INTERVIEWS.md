# Interview Guide — Tasks 1–6

How this works: answer one task's batch at a time, in any session (web or
desktop), by typing or voice memo, in any order — though Task 1 is the urgent
one. Whichever session receives answers fills in the `A:` lines, follows up on
anything interesting, distills decisions into the plan doc, and pushes.
Skip any question by writing "skip".
