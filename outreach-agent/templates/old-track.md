# OLD track — recently-closed / stale posting ("did you ever fill this?")

Goal: low-pressure, curious, complimentary. The role was posted a while ago and may
still be unfilled. Eric is raising his hand, not hard-selling.

Structure (write naturally, ~80–110 words):
- **First line (custom):** reference the specific role and that it was posted a while back.
  e.g. "I saw you were hiring for a [role] a few months ago — did you ever fill it?"
- **One genuine, specific compliment** about the company / their content / their product.
- **Soft value:** if they're still looking (or it comes up again), Eric does end-to-end
  on-camera content — research, scripting, delivery. One line, not a brochure.
- **CTA:** offer the reel + a quick chat. Low commitment.

Tone: warm, human, no urgency. It's fine to acknowledge the role may be filled.
LinkedIn note: friendly, references the old posting, <300 chars.
